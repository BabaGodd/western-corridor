TRAIN SOUNDS (this folder sits next to index.html)

Bundled sounds (generated for this project, not field recordings):
  train-loop.wav       seamless 12 s loop: diesel engine drone + wheel/rail clatter. Speed follows the train.
  train-horn.wav       locomotive horn (long - long - short - long), played as the train nears each station
  station-arrive.wav   brake squeal + air release, played when the train stops

To use REAL recordings instead, add files with the same names but .mp3 or .ogg
(train-loop.mp3, train-horn.mp3, station-arrive.mp3). They are used in preference to the .wav files.
Use recordings you are licensed to use. Make loops seamless (no gap at the join).
Press M in the app to mute / unmute.
